application questions (phase 5 -- e.g. "What's your salary expectation?",
"Are you legally authorized to work in Canada?", "What's your notice
period?"). Copy this file to `applicant_notes.md` (gitignored -- it will
hold real personal details) and fill in what's true for you. Leave a line
out entirely if you'd rather answer it yourself; drafted answers only use
what you actually state here, never invent a number or status.
